# R Data Visualization Projects

Coursework projects in R (RStudio, R Markdown) on data visualization: two analysis reports and two visualization critiques.

## Projects

### 1. Global GDP Growth Analysis (World Bank data)
Tracks how national GDP (current US$) changed over time across regions, comparing emerging economies with developed nations. Built with ggplot2: line plot, bar chart, and box plot.
- Data: World Bank, World Development Indicators (GDP), 1960-2023, over 200 countries and regional aggregates
- Files: `Global GDP Growth Analysis.Rmd`, `gdp_visualization_report.html`

### 2. Diamond Attributes and Pricing
Exploratory analysis of the ggplot2 `diamonds` dataset (53,940 diamonds): carat, cut, color, clarity, and price, shown through eight distinct graphics across multiple figure types.
- Files: `diamond_attribute_dataVisualization.Rmd`, `diamond_attribute_dataVisualization.html`

### 3. Visualization Critique: Declining Birth Rates Globally
Critique of a Financial Times chart on global fertility trends.
- Source: John Burn-Murdoch, "Why Birth Rates Are Falling Everywhere All at Once", Financial Times, May 2026 (discussed on FlowingData: https://flowingdata.com/2026/05/25/declining-birth-rates-globally/)

### 4. Visualization Critique: Global Renewable Energy
Critique of an interactive Our World in Data visualization on renewable energy.
- Source: https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/electricity-mix?source=renewables&metric=share_of_generation&frequency=annual

## Tools
R, RStudio, R Markdown, ggplot2, plotly, dplyr, tidyverse
